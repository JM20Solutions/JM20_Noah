We collect information you provide directly: name, company name, email address, phone number, and website. When operating managed services, we may process your customers' data strictly on your instructions and within the scope of our Data Processing Agreement.

Your contact information is used solely to respond to your enquiry, deliver our services, and send relevant updates. We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

All data is stored within EU infrastructure (Supabase, Frankfurt region). We do not transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA without appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses).

5. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your personal data. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@jm20.eu.

This website uses Google Analytics to measure how the site is used, but only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Your choice is stored in your browser so it can be remembered, and you can change it at any time from “Cookie settings” in the footer. Any other data stored locally is session state required for the website to function.
